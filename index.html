ThriftedByVic

I started it at 16 to make a few extra dollars. It turned into a full operation once I realized data drives decisions.

Now I walk through Goodwill and thrift stores doing math before anything touches the cart. If I can't see a piece reselling for at least 3x what I'd pay, it stays on the rack. A $5 find has to become a $15 sale to come home with me.

I list it across Depop, Mercari, and Poshmark, and later I put a SQL database behind it. A sample of 114 sales showed what I'd felt on the ground: Mercari prices ran higher, but Depop moved most of the volume.

Does Education Level Predict Earnings?

A regression study of Census PSEO earnings in Pennsylvania. I wrote the SQL that cleaned 10,978 records, cutting 185 fields of study to 20 majors and removing suppressed data. Degree level alone explained 27% of Year 10 earnings variance, rising to 85% once major was controlled. I later rebuilt it in Python so it reruns outside Excel.
