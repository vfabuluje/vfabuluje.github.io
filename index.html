The story

I left on purpose, moving across the country for school because I felt too comfortable, and I've never found much growth in comfortable places. I'm now a senior at Penn State Harrisburg studying Computer Science with a concentration in Data Science. Featured project · Oct 2026 – Present

Canary: a bank-failure early-warning system.

Can public filings warn you a bank is in trouble before it fails?

I built a warehouse of 25 years of FDIC filings (710K bank-quarters) and trained an XGBoost model on 31 CAMELS-style ratios to rank banks by failure risk. It was trained on 2001–2014 and tested once on 2016–2024, and it flagged 72% of failed banks, most 9–12 months ahead.

It missed Silicon Valley Bank. I diagnosed why and built a screen that would have caught it about 10 months early. It was built after the fact, so it's a diagnosis, not a validated result.
